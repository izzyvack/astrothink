Frequently asked questions

What ages are the programs for?

Our core space and AI activities are for younger students. Other programs serve middle and high school students. The programs page lists the age group for each offering.

Does every program cost the same?

No. Python & Neural Networks, Beyond the Stethoscope, and Career Pathways were offered free in Summer 2026. Ask us about pricing and availability for future sessions and school programs.

How do I register for the next session?

Our published Summer 2026 sessions have concluded. Email us or check Instagram for information about future sessions.

Do I need an account to explore?

You can use the parachute lab on this site without an account. Some tools in the existing AstroThink learning portal require a separate portal account.
