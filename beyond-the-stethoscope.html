Beyond the Stethoscope

A three-session healthcare exploration program created with Aurora Public Library. Students explore healthcare careers, the body's defenses, and new medical technology through case studies and group problem-solving.

Summer 2026 session concluded

Who it’s for

Rising grades 6–9

Format

Aurora Public Library

Length

3 sessions

Cost

Free

Program topics

Middle school students explore healthcare careers, the immune system, and new medical technology.

  1. Inside healthcare

    Explore healthcare settings, the people who work in them, and a patient's journey through care.

  2. The science of staying healthy

    Learn about bacteria, viruses, and the immune system, then work through a case study.

  3. The future of medicine

    Explore new technology, AI, and machine learning in medicine before presenting a final case study.
