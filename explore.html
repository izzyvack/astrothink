A simplified model with constant air density, a fully open canopy, and quadratic drag. The animation runs at real time.

Earth gravity: 9.81 m/s² / Air density: 1.225 kg/m³ / Drag coefficient: 1.5

How the model works

Gravity pulls the payload down. Air resistance pushes against the motion. A larger canopy catches more air, increasing drag. A heavier payload needs a greater drag force to balance its weight.

When those forces balance, the parachute reaches its terminal speed. Try keeping the mass fixed while changing the canopy diameter.

Suggested experiment

Double the canopy diameter while keeping the mass the same. The canopy area becomes four times larger. In this model, that halves the terminal speed.
