Space & AI

Our core program brings space science and artificial intelligence to younger students through hands-on activities, demonstrations, and projects. Students explore ideas by making things and trying them out.

Program topics

Students explore space science and artificial intelligence through practical activities.

  1. Space science

    Explore rockets, parachutes, and the engineering behind space exploration.

  2. Artificial intelligence

    Get a first look at how computers learn from information and how people use AI.

  3. Hands-on projects

    Build, test, and change a design to see what happens.

Session information

Programs have included sessions at Welch Elementary and the Alive Center. Ages, dates, and cost depend on the location. Contact the team for the next available session.
