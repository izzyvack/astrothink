Career Pathways

A five-week speaker program for students thinking about high school and what comes next. Guests share their work and answer questions about different fields, including engineering, law, research, and writing.

Summer 2026 session concluded

Who it’s for

Grades 7–9

Format

95th Street Library, Naperville

Length

5 sessions

Cost

Free

Program topics

Students meet guest speakers and ask questions about their work and education.

  1. Different fields

    Hear about the daily work behind careers you may have considered, and discover others.

  2. Real conversations

    Ask guest speakers about their experiences, decisions, and routes into their fields.

  3. Your own interests

    Use what you hear to think about the subjects and opportunities you want to explore.
